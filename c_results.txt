=====================================================================
3. SUMMARY OF FINDINGS
=====================================================================
- Thresholds: in every scheme, authorized sets recover the secret and
  unauthorized sets cannot (Scheme III: all 8 Hamming subsets and all 128
  Golay subsets checked).
- Perfectness: all four binary codes (Hamming [7,4], [31,26], [127,120] and
  Golay [23,12]) passed the run-time perfect-code check.
- Golay secret space: 4094 of 4096 codewords, eta = 0.9995.
- Information rates: Scheme I rho = k/(n-1): 1.143 (n=8,t=2), 2.000
  (n=10,t=3), 2.714 (n=8,t=4). Scheme IV (n=8,t=4): 0.571 < 1.
  Scheme II rho = t. Scheme III rho = k/log2(m): 1.42 to 17.17.
- Tampering: Scheme I accepts a wrong secret when the tampered edge goes to
  a non-member of the coalition; Scheme IV is unaffected. A tampered edge
  between two coalition members is detected in both schemes.
- Timing: Scheme I/IV reconstruction grows ~5.0x from k=183 to k=316
  (cubic prediction: 5.1x). Scheme II is dominated by share generation.
  Scheme III is the cheapest (table-lookup decoding).
